Privacy Policy

Zumbizera · Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Zumbizera game handles information when you use it. The game is designed for multiplayer entertainment and uses Google Firebase services to support gameplay.

1. Registration and Personal Information

Zumbizera does not require conventional registration and does not ask players to provide their full name, email address, phone number, home address, personal identification documents, or payment information to use the game.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, or address book.

2. Anonymous Authentication

The game uses Firebase Authentication, provided by Google, through its anonymous authentication feature. This service creates an anonymous account and a unique technical identifier (UID) to authenticate the player and support multiplayer rooms.

This identifier does not, by itself, represent the player's name, email address, or phone number. The UID is used for game operations and may be stored in multiplayer room records in Cloud Firestore.

3. Username

Players may choose a username (nickname) to use in the game. The nickname is checked by an automatic filter for prohibited words and is stored only on the player's device using the app's local storage (LocalStorage).

The nickname is not sent to Cloud Firestore and is not used to create an account containing the player's real-world identity. We recommend that players do not use personal information as their nickname.

4. Multiplayer Room Data

Zumbizera uses Google Cloud Firestore to create and manage public and private multiplayer rooms. For this purpose, technical information related to rooms may be processed and stored, including:

  • Room code or identifier;
  • Host technical identifier (hostPeerId);
  • Host's anonymous Firebase UID (hostUid);
  • Host activity information (hostHeartbeat);
  • Number of players (playerCount);
  • Room status (status);
  • Room creation date and time (createdAt);
  • Whether the room is public or private (isPublic).

This information is used to create, find, authenticate access to, and operate multiplayer rooms. The room data stored in Firestore does not include the player's nickname.

5. Communication Between Players

Zumbizera does not provide text chat, voice chat, messaging, emoji sending, or other content-sharing features between players through these means.

6. Third-Party Services

The game uses services provided by Google Firebase, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. When these services are used, technical information necessary for authentication, security, and infrastructure operation may be processed by Google, subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

For more information, please see:

  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Privacy Policy

The developer does not sell players' data or share it with third parties for targeted advertising.

7. Data Storage and Security

The nickname is stored locally on the player's device. Technical identifiers and data required for multiplayer rooms are processed through Firebase services.

Cloud Firestore security rules are used to control access to room information. However, no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Advertising and Tracking

Zumbizera does not use the information described in this policy to build advertising profiles or track player behavior for targeted advertising.

9. Children's Privacy

Zumbizera does not ask players to provide personal information to create an account or play the game. Children's use of the game should comply with the app store's age rating and applicable guidance from parents or legal guardians.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the game, the services it uses, or applicable legal requirements. The current version will be available on this page, together with its latest update date.
